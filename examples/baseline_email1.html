Below is an evidence-led update on adjuvant therapy choices in resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, based on the most recent Phase 3 readout.

Adjuvant therapy that delayed recurrence in resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC

In a Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled study, adjuvant therapy reduced the risk of disease recurrence or death (HR 0.66; 95% CI: 0.55–0.81)1.

Designed to restore T-cell anti-tumor activity

Blockade of the PD-1 / PD-L1 axis reactivates exhausted tumor-infiltrating T cells, restoring an immune response against residual disease2.
